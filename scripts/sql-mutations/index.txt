name: search-query-unquoted
file: src/memory/index.rs
search: format!("\"{}\"", t.replace('"', "\"\""))
replace: t.to_string()
expect: survive
why: Equivalent today: tokens are lowercased alphanumerics, and FTS5 operators (AND, OR, NOT, NEAR) are uppercase-only barewords. Quoting guards against a future tokenizer that keeps punctuation or case.
